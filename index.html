Biography

Venkat Tej (Penubothu Venkat) is an Indian filmmaker and screenwriter active in Telugu Cinema since 2014. He began his mainstream journey as an Associate Director on the acclaimed espionage thriller Goodachari (Abhishek Pictures & People Media Factory), before transitioning into prestige writers' rooms with Arka Media Works on Netflix's Baahubali: Before the Beginning and Disney+ Hotstar's Parampara (Seasons 1 & 2).

Across collaborations with 14 Reels Plus Entertainment, A.K. Entertainments, and independent directors, Venkat has contributed to over a dozen theatrical features and OTT originals while earning 10 international screenplay awards including the Dadasaheb Phalke International Film Festival, Prix Royal Paris, and Sacramento International Film Festival.
