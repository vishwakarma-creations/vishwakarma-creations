Filmmaker Biography

Venkat Tej (a.k.a. Penubothu Venkat) is an Indian filmmaker and screenwriter known for his work across Telugu theatrical cinema and premier pan-Indian OTT originals. Beginning his industry journey as an Associate Director on the espionage thriller Goodachari (2018), he transitioned into flagship writers’ rooms with Arka Media Works on Netflix’s Baahubali: Before the Beginning, Disney+ Hotstar’s hit drama Parampara (Seasons 1 & 2), and feature development Malawi.

He went on to serve as Associate Screenwriter across multiple OTT series and feature films for 14 Reels Plus Entertainment (Ghost Lives, Brand Ka Band Baja, Rangoon Raja Ratnam Trophy, Green Gold, Abhay), A.K. Entertainments (HIStory), and filmmaker Maggidi RamaKrishna (Saptah), while earning 10 international screenplay festival wins before founding Vishwakarma Creations.
